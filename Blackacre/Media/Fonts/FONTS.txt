Fonts bundled with In Character Forever
=======================================

These fonts are not covered by the add-on's MIT license. Each belongs to its
designer and is included unmodified, under the free terms its designer
publishes. Thank you to every designer below.

Dwarven Stonecraft (DWARVESC.TTF)
    Copyright rzy/SD (SDFonts), 2000. Free for personal and commercial use.

Freebooter (FREEBOOTERUPDATED.TTF)
    Copyright GemFonts (Graham Meade), 1998. Free for personal and commercial use.

Monarchia (MONARCHI.TTF)
    Petra; compiled by GemFonts (Graham Meade). Free for personal and commercial use.

Magic School One (MagicSchoolOne-ovYz.ttf)
    Copyright (c) 2004 Michael Hagemann, FontMesa (www.fontmesa.com).
    Free for personal and commercial use.

Burning Wrath (b_wrath.ttf)
    By T. Lyle (rubicant.com). Released as free.

Darnassian Runes (DarnassianRunes-Regular_2.ttf)
Thalassian (Thalassian_Font.ttf)
Shalassian (ShalassianFont-Regular.ttf)
    By Feyawen, Warcraft Fonts (warcraftfonts.carrd.co), an unofficial fan
    project. Darnassian Runes is published as 100% free.

The journal's other font choices (Friz, Morpheus, Skurri and the default)
use the copies that come with World of Warcraft itself; none are bundled.

If you designed one of these fonts and want it credited differently or
removed, please open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
